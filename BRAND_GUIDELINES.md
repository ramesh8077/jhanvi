# Jhanvi Mishra Design: Brand & Aesthetic Guidelines

## Core Philosophy
- **Aim:** To revolutionize the interior design world in India by introducing authentic, deeply influenced European design aesthetics.
- **Design Language:** European embedded designs. A blend of structural rigor, historical romance, and modern luxury.
- **Work Ethics & Style:** High-end, sophisticated, culturally rich, and meticulously detailed.

## Global Exposure (The 15+ Countries Influence)
The design sensibility is directly drawn from extensive travels and architectural studies across:
- Germany
- Switzerland
- Belgium
- Prague (Czech Republic)
- Austria
- Malta
- UK
- Netherlands
- Paris (France)
- Luxembourg
- Portugal
- Turkey
- Poland
- Italy (Specifically Milano, Venice)

## Social Media (Instagram) & Visuals
All visuals, posts, and UI must reflect this European journey—using elegant typography, muted but rich color palettes, and a focus on craftsmanship and architectural heritage.
